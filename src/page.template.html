What you are looking at

4,704 little metronomes, each swinging at its own speed and tuned by position — low on the left, high on the right. Each one nudges its four neighbours, so a disturbance you make spreads outward as a wave. Bright means recently set in motion.

Drag anywhere. You will paint a bright trail and hear notes; higher up the screen is higher pitch. Try Burst to set every cell going at once.

The Order figure stays near zero and that is correct — the grid spans many pitches, so it organises into local waves rather than one unison. Local order is the number that responds to Coupling.

Tap Enable audio — browsers require a gesture before sound.

Drag to conduct · tap Hide for a full-size field

Physics

Coupling is the interesting dial: at 0 the grid is chaos, mid-range it forms travelling waves and spirals. Each column is tuned to a different pitch, so neighbouring columns can agree without the whole grid ever unifying — watch the waves glow, not the Order number.

How to play

  • Drag on the field to push waves through it.
  • Drag with the button held to also sound notes along the pitch axis.
  • Right-click drag to damp the field instead of exciting it.
  • Home row asdf… / number row q2w3e… play scale degrees.
  • Space pause · B burst · C silence · M mutate · N reseed · R reset
